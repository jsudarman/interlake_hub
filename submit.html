Guidelines for Club Officers

Post Update
  • Agendas — submit before the meeting. Helps members plan and keeps absent students in the loop.
  • Recaps — submit within 2 days. Include highlights, decisions, and next steps.
  • Announcements — anytime for deadlines, competition results, or club news.
Edit Profile
  • Keep meeting details up to date so interested students always know when and where to show up.
  • Update the officer list each year after elections. Add a leadership photo to personalize your page.
  • Changes are live the moment you hit Save — no approval needed.
