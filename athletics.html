📋 Eligibility Requirements
✓
Must be enrolled at Interlake High School and in good academic standing (WIAA minimum: 1.0 GPA / no more than one failing grade per semester).
✓
Valid ASB card required — purchase at the main office or online through the school store.
✓
BSD Sports Physical must be on file with the Athletics & Activities department before the first practice. Physicals are valid for two years.
✓
ImPACT baseline concussion test required for contact sports (Football, Basketball, Soccer, Wrestling, Lacrosse) before the first practice.
✓
No outstanding fines, unpaid fees, or overdue library books. Clear obligations in the main office before tryouts.
✓
Student must not have exceeded the WIAA maximum of 8 semesters of high school athletic eligibility.

How to Get Cleared
1
Get a sports physical — schedule with your doctor. BSD physicals are valid for 2 years.
2
Submit physical to the Athletics office — bring the signed form to the main office or drop off in the athletics room.
3
Purchase your ASB card — available at the student store or main office.
4
Complete ImPACT test (contact sports only) — done at school before your first practice.
5
Pay participation fees — check with the main office for your sport's fee amount.
