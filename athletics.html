How to Participate in a Sport
1
Register online through IHS FinalForms — complete all required forms and waivers.

International, BVA, or Big Picture students: contact Mrs. Higgins
2
Complete ImPACT baseline testing if required for your sport. Contact Mrs. Higgins or Lauren to schedule — takes 30–45 minutes.
higginsk@bsd405.org · olsonla@bsd405.org
3
Submit a BSD sports physical to the Athletics & Activities department. Upload in FinalForms or email to Mrs. Higgins.
4
Pay participation fees through the district payment portal. ASB membership is required — fees applied when team rosters are complete.
