What is ASB?

The Associated Student Body (ASB) is Interlake's official student government. Elected officers represent all students, plan and run school events, promote school spirit, and serve as the link between students and administration.

ASB oversees Homecoming, Spirit Week, dances, pep assemblies, and community service initiatives — managing the student activities budget and coordinating with clubs, classes, and staff year-round.
