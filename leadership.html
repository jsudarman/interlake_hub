📣 ASB Announcements
🏈 Homecoming
  • Homecoming is September 25–26! Assembly and football game on Thursday the 25th, dance on Friday the 26th. This is the biggest week of the school year — don't miss it.
  • Homecoming theme announcement coming soon. Follow @InterlakeASB on Instagram to be the first to know.
  • Dance tickets go on sale soon through the school store. Pre-sale: $15 · At the door: $20. You must present your ASB card for entry.
  • Homecoming court nominations open September 5th. Nomination forms are available in the main office. Nominate a peer who represents Interlake's values!
  • Guest passes are required for any non-Interlake student attending the dance. Forms must be submitted to the main office by September 19th — no exceptions.
🗳️ Freshman Class Office Elections
  • Freshman class officer elections are happening this September! All freshmen are eligible to run for President, Vice President, Secretary, or Treasurer.
  • Want to run? Pick up a candidate packet from the main office. Deadline to submit: September 10th. Don't wait — spots fill fast!
  • Candidate speeches will be held September 18th during advisory period in the main gym. All freshmen are encouraged to attend and support their peers.
  • Voting is September 19th. All freshmen will receive a voting link to their school email — check your inbox and make your voice heard!
  • Results announced September 22nd. Congratulations in advance to Interlake's first-ever Class of 2030 officers!
🎉 Assemblies
  • Welcoming Back Assembly — September 4th. The Crossroads Cup kicks off! Each grade competes for the trophy. Show up in your grade color: Seniors — blue, Juniors — gold, Sophomores — white, Freshmen — green.
  • Homecoming Assembly — September 25th. Cheer on your class during competitions, watch the Homecoming court presentation, and get hyped for the football game that night.
  • All assemblies are held in the main gym. Arrive on time — doors close at the bell. Late entry is not permitted once the assembly begins.

What is ASB?

The Associated Student Body (ASB) is Interlake's official student government. Elected officers represent all students, plan and run school events, promote school spirit, and serve as the link between students and administration.

ASB oversees Homecoming, Spirit Week, dances, pep assemblies, and community service initiatives — managing the student activities budget and coordinating with clubs, classes, and staff year-round.
